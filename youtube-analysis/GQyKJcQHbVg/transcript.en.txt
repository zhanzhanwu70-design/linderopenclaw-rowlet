Today, I would like

our latest accessory,
our alignment aid.
The alignment aid is
a converted light curtain
which helps you
to visualize
the scan plane
of the scanner.
The alignment
aid consists of
21 IR receiver elements
which are located 15
millimeters apart.
When IR light is detected,
the LEDs turn red.
The grreat thing is: It is now
possible to visualize
the scan plane
of the scanner
also at large distances.
Therefore, it is easy
to adjust
the mounting position
of the scanner.
Additionally,
you also have
an aid indicator on
the alignment bar itself.
The alignment bar
is battery-powered.
You can easily charge
the alignment bar with
a Mini-USB cable.
It is very portable.
Due to its compact size,
it's easy to put
the alignment bar in
a bagpack or bag.
And most importantly:
It is quiet.
