### Top 5 takeaways to remember

1. **U-shape is real and bad.** On 20-doc multi-doc QA, GPT-3.5-Turbo-16K drops from 75.7% at position 1 to 54.1% at position 10 — a 21-point swing, and the middle is *below* the no-document baseline of 56.1%. Same for Claude-1.3 (62.9% → 56.8% → 60.1%), MPT-30B-Instruct, LongChat-13B.

2. **Bigger context ≠ better use of context.** When input fits in both GPT-3.5 (4K) and GPT-3.5-16K, the curves are nearly superimposed. Claude-1.3 vs. Claude-1.3-100K — same. The 16K / 100K model did not learn to use more, it just got more room.

3. **It's a retrieval failure, not a reasoning failure.** Synthetic key-value retrieval with random UUIDs (no semantics to reason about) still shows the U for GPT-3.5 / MPT / LongChat. So the model can *fail to locate* the right token in the prompt, even before any "reasoning" comes in.

4. **The U is scale-emergent, not SFT-induced.** Comparing MPT-30B base vs. MPT-30B-Instruct: both U-shaped. Llama-2 sweep: 7B is purely recency-biased (no primacy at all), 13B/70B recover primacy, and SFT+RLHF only marginally narrows the gap.

5. **Open-domain QA reader saturates before retriever does.** NaturalQuestions + Contriever, k ∈ {5,10,20,30,40,50}. Reader accuracy plateaus by k=20; 20→50 is only +1.5% for GPT-3.5 / +1% for Claude. **Top-k is a trade-off, not a free knob.** Rerank + truncation > bigger top-k.

### Why this matters for code agents (OpenClaw relevance)

- Position in the prompt is a **first-class hyperparameter** — current-task evidence at the start, exact identifiers / error codes / log lines / policy text **not** buried in a long transcript.
- Top-k is not "more is better" — measure reader accuracy vs. k and find the saturation knee; rerank aggressively.
- Query-aware prompt wrapping (put the task statement at the top **and** bottom of the context block) is almost free and fixes the simplest retrieval failures.
- The U is one of the design pressures behind the whole memory-hierarchy / externalized-history / interleaved-retrieval wave (MemGPT, Self-RAG, ReSum). The architectural responses in the wiki all exist *because* of this empirical finding.
- Per-model-family assumptions don't transfer — encoder-decoder models are flat within training length. Be careful if/when OpenClaw supports enc-dec models.
